## What to look for
New model releases with capability comparisons to prior version or competitors. Open-weight releases that shift the enterprise build-vs-buy calculus. Significant API pricing changes (direct impact on internal deployment economics).

## Scoring notes
Lead question: "Is this a meaningfully more capable or cheaper model than what existed last week?" Score high for benchmark reversals, dramatic price cuts (e.g. 80% cost reduction), or first-ever capability demonstrations with enterprise applicability. Generic release announcements without a clear capability leap score 1-3.
