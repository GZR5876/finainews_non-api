## What to look for
Security or hallucination incidents in deployed enterprise agents (high risk signal for CFO). Significant funding rounds for AI companies with port/logistics/finance use cases.

## Scoring notes
Score highest for incidents (agent caused an error, data loss, unexpected action) or dramatic productivity gains at a named company. Generic agentic AI announcements without deployment evidence score 1-3.
