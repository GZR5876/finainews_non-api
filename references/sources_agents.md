## What to look for
- New agentic AI products capable of autonomous multi-step workflows in enterprise
- AI agents for financial reporting, board pack generation, audit trail creation
- Agents integrated into ERP/TMS/WMS systems used in port logistics
- Supply chain visibility or trade documentation agents (bills of lading, customs)
- Significant funding rounds for AI companies with port/logistics/finance use cases
- Security or hallucination incidents in deployed enterprise agents (risk signal)

## Scoring notes
Score highest when an agent is deployed in production at a named enterprise with
measurable outcome. Score down for demo-stage products or research previews
with no clear deployment timeline.
