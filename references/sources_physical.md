## What to look for
- Autonomous terminal equipment deployments or contracts (AGVs, automated stacking cranes)
- AI-driven berth/vessel scheduling tools going live at peer ports
- Drone inspection programmes replacing manual surveying
- Incidents or recalls that signal safety/liability risk for operators
- AI/automation capex disclosures from port operator peers
- Concession, tariff, or labour developments tied to terminal automation

## Scoring notes
Score highest when tied to a named port deployment, quantified productivity gain,
or a capital procurement event. Peer operator disclosures score at full value.
Generic robotics R&D scores lower unless it has a clear port logistics application
within 2 years.
