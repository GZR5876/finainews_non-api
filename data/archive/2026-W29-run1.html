Section 02

The Acronyms, Decoded

LLM
Large Language Model - The AI “brain” that enables AI tools to understand and generate human language.
Model
A specific implementation of the LLM technology, that uses what it has learned from its training data to understand requests and generate responses.
Instructions
A set of rules that tells the AI how to perform the task.
Prompt
A specific request that tells the AI what task to perform.
Token
The small pieces of text (roughly word fragments) the AI reads and writes. Usage is billed in tokens, like a phone plan counts minutes.
Tools
Actions an agent is allowed to take beyond chatting: searching the web, reading files, running calculations, sending emails.
Context Window
The AI's working memory: how much text it can keep in mind at once. Past that limit, it starts losing track of earlier material.
MCP
Model Context Protocol — a universal plug (think USB) that lets an AI safely connect to your files, systems, and applications.
Agent
An AI that doesn't just answer questions — it takes steps on its own: planning a task, using tools, and working until the job is done.
AGI
Artificial General Intelligence — a future AI as capable as a person at virtually any task. It does not exist yet.
Memory
What the AI remembers about you between conversations — preferences, past work — so you don't have to repeat yourself every time.
Knowledge
Reference documents you give the AI — policies, reports, manuals — so answers draw on your material, not just its general training.
Hallucination
When the AI gives a confident, polished answer that is simply wrong or made up. Always double-check facts and figures.
Grounding
Making the AI base its answers on trusted, checkable sources rather than guessing — the main defence against hallucination.
Skill
A pre-packaged how-to that teaches an agent to do one specific task well — like a recipe card it pulls out when needed.
HITL (Human in the Loop)
A person reviews and approves the AI's work at key checkpoints before anything is final — the AI drafts, a human decides.
