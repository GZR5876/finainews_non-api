# AI Weekly — Port CFO Briefing · 27 May–10 June 2026

*Week of 8 June 2026 · Issue 2026-W24-run1*

This week's pattern: AI keeps getting cheaper and more capable, while Gartner finds only **7% of finance teams** see high impact from it so far.

---

## 1. AI in Finance

Vale automates finance and tax workflows with Databricks agents

Mining group **Vale** now uses AI agents to check its insurance policies and screen **more than 10,000 tax laws**, replacing manual review work.

Source: https://www.databricks.com/customers/vale/agent-bricks

PwC launches Claude-native finance practice with Anthropic

**PwC** is packaging Anthropic's Claude into **ready-made AI assistants** for finance teams, its second such practice after one built with OpenAI.

Source: https://www.pwc.com/us/en/about-us/newsroom/press-releases/pwc-anthropic-ai-native-finance-life-sciences-enterprise-agents.html

Gartner says CFOs mistake finance AI deployment for value creation

Gartner finds almost every finance team has AI in place or planned, yet only **7% see high impact**, a warning against spending without measurement.

Source: https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-28-gartner-says-cfos-must-stop-mistaking-finance-ai-deployment-for-value-creation

EY and Microsoft commit US$1B+ to scale enterprise AI

EY and Microsoft are putting **over US$1 billion** behind moving clients from AI pilots to full rollout, with finance work early on the list.

Source: https://www.ey.com/en_ca/newsroom/2026/05/ey-and-microsoft-announce-global-initiative-to-help-clients-scale-ai-enterprise-wide-value-creation-and-move-beyond-experimentation
