## 1. AI in Finance

Vale automates finance and tax workflows with Databricks agents

Mining and logistics group **Vale** runs Databricks agents across finance and tax, supporting **100+ users** and screening **10,000+ tax laws** and 430+ insurance policies.

Source: https://www.databricks.com/customers/vale/agent-bricks

PwC launches Claude-native finance practice with Anthropic

**PwC's AI Native Finance** group builds regulated finance skills, connectors and plugins on **Claude**, spanning Cowork and Claude Code, mirroring its OpenAI-native finance build.

Source: https://www.pwc.com/us/en/about-us/newsroom/press-releases/pwc-anthropic-ai-native-finance-life-sciences-enterprise-agents.html

Gartner says CFOs mistake finance AI deployment for value creation

Source: https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-28-gartner-says-cfos-must-stop-mistaking-finance-ai-deployment-for-value-creation

EY and Microsoft commit US$1B+ to scale enterprise AI

The **US$1 billion-plus** EY and Microsoft initiative industrialises enterprise AI adoption; EY cites finance pilots cutting lead times **95%** and operating costs **37%**.

Source: https://www.ey.com/en_ca/newsroom/2026/05/ey-and-microsoft-announce-global-initiative-to-help-clients-scale-ai-enterprise-wide-value-creation-and-move-beyond-experimentation
