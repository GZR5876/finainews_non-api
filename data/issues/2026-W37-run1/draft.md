*Covering 14 August to 13 September 2026*

## 1. AI in Finance

OpenAI's finance team cut close reporting from days to hours

OpenAI's finance team used its own **Codex** agent to cut compute-cost reconciliation from **five days to five hours**, aiming for a zero-day close.

Source: https://www.cfodive.com/news/inside-openai-experiment-ai-coding-finance/829250/

Google Cloud launches Gemini Enterprise for Financial Services

Google Cloud's new platform links agents to **FactSet, LSEG and Moody's** data, cutting bond portfolio risk analysis to **under five minutes**.

Source: https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/introducing-gemini-enterprise-for-financial-services

Gartner: CFOs must pilot governance first before scaling AI agents

Gartner says CFOs should judge AI agent pilots by **governance readiness**, not ROI, requiring a **complete failure log** for every run.

Source: https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/new/pr-q-a-new-vis-template/2026-08-20-gartner-says-cfos-mus-pilot-governance-first-before-scaling-ai-agents

AI forces finance leaders to take on tech responsibilities

Deloitte surveyed **1,434 finance leaders** across 26 countries; **54%** now own cross-enterprise AI and technology capital-allocation decisions outside traditional finance scope.

Source: https://www.cfobrew.com/stories/ai-forces-finance-leaders-to-take-on-tech-responsibilities
