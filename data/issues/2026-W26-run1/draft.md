## 1. AI in Finance

**KPMG pulled its agentic AI report after hallucinated citations were exposed**

> **GPTZero found only 5 of 45 citations in KPMG's agentic AI report pointed to real sources**; KPMG pulled the report on June 13, acknowledging its own responsible-AI guidelines on human oversight had not been followed. Any AI-generated board paper or regulatory filing your team produces or receives needs the same citation check.
>
> Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/13/kpmg-pulls-report-on-ai-usage-due-to-apparent-hallucinations/

> Source: https://www.bottomline.com/newsroom/press-releases/bottomline-unveils-cfo-suite-bring-together-governed-ai-and-end-end-cash-flow-management
